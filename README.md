# Éverton MuVi

---

My career path is marked by the constant pursuit of the synergy between technology and wisdom. Guided by a structured philosophical thought and a keen intuition, I seek, on this art, to conceive, develop, and collaborate in the implementation of innovative solutions across a wide range of platforms and fields of application. My approach is characterized by a holistic perspective, capable of analyzing challenges from concrete technical aspects to the most abstract concepts. My work consists of facilitating technology and information in the pursuit of knowledge, intelligence, and wisdom. My motto is "From Technology to Wisdom: Solutions with Knowledge and Intelligence through the Power of Information and Consciousness". With extensive experience in both the public and private sectors, a solid academic background, and substantial continuous learning, always aiming a high level of competence in both conception and leadership, integrating high management into consistent projects.
