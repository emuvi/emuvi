# Éverton MuVi

---

My career path is defined by a constant pursuit of synergy between technology and wisdom. Guided by structured philosophical thinking and keen intuition, I aim to conceive, develop, and collaborate on the implementation of innovative solutions across a wide range of platforms and fields of application. My approach is characterized by a holistic perspective, capable of analyzing challenges ranging from concrete technical aspects to the most abstract concepts. My work focuses on leveraging technology and information to foster knowledge, intelligence, and wisdom. My motto is "From Technology to Wisdom: Solutions driven by Knowledge and Intelligence through the Power of Information and Consciousness." I bring extensive experience in both the public and private sectors, a solid academic background, and a commitment to continuous learning, all aimed at achieving a high level of competence in conceiving and leading robust projects.
